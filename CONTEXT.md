# Medius — Build Rules (read before every task)
ONE RULE: The AI never decides anything. It interviews, explains, simplifies, translates, drafts, and organizes. Every real decision is made by a human — the two parties or a human mediator. The AI may PREPARE actions but has ZERO commit permission; a human always presses the button.
GUARDRAILS:
- Citation lockbox: output may ONLY cite statutes retrieved this call; show the real retrieved text by every citation.
- Scope disclosure: every analysis output includes a "not_considered" note.
- Human-approval gate: nothing reaches the other party or is finalized without an explicit human approval flag.
- Merits firewall: having a lawyer affects ONLY triage priority/support, NEVER legal-merits scores.
- No prohibited features: never predict who wins, never score credibility, never auto-settle/auto-close.
ACCESSIBILITY RULES:
- Every AI explanation is produced at THREE reading levels at once and cached: simple (~4th grade), standard (~8th grade), full (raw statute text + citations). Default shown = simple; deeper levels are one tap away.
- Never dead-end on a missing document: support no-document, photographed-document (OCR), and verbal-description intake paths.
- Intake is a guided interview of short/yes-no questions; users never have to write a paragraph.
- "What This Means For Me" summaries answer only: what the law says about my situation, what I can do (options), what the risks are — in plain language, NO win/loss prediction, NO "you should", always paired with "this is information, not legal advice."
- Trust signaling is always visible: an "AI didn't decide this" label on AI output, a plain-language privacy line, a "what we won't do" list, and a persistent button to reach a human.
